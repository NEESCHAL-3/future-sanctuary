OUR SHARED HOME

Planet Earth in 2026

Standing in 2026 at age 21, I look upon our spinning blue planet not as a resource to exploit, but as a sacred home we hold in trust for future generations. Over the past years, humanity initiated the largest environmental restoration and clean energy efforts in history.

01

Planetary Healing & Reforestation

From 2020 to 2026, over 40 billion native trees were planted worldwide. Critical rainforest reserves, ocean sanctuaries, and river basins were permanently granted constitutional protection.

02

Universal Education & Empathy

Digital divide barriers collapsed. Every student, regardless of region, gained access to open-source learning libraries, mental health resources, and collaborative problem-solving spaces.

03

Clean Energy & Human Dignity

Solar, wind, and next-generation fusion grids achieved grid parity across six continents. Innovation shifted away from profit-seeking toward universal human flourishing and healthcare equity.
